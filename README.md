# Privacy Policy — Untold Devotion

*Last updated: 3 July 2026*

> **Short version:** This policy covers every app and game published by Untold Devotion on Google Play (and any other store we release on). None of our apps collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. They have no internet connection requirement, no analytics, no advertising, and no account system. Anything an app remembers — such as your high score, level progress, or in-app settings — stays only on your own device.

## 1. Who this policy covers

This is the privacy policy for **Untold Devotion** ("we", "us", "our"), covering all apps and games we publish, currently and in the future, across Google Play and any other distribution platform we use. Where an individual app has its own listing, this same policy applies to it unless that app's store listing links to a separate, app-specific policy.

## 2. Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data through our apps. Specifically, our apps do not collect, request, or transmit:

- Your name, email address, or any contact details
- Your location
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
- Photos, contacts, or any other data from your device
- Payment or financial information (purchases are handled entirely by the app store you downloaded from — we never see your payment details)

## 3. Information stored locally on your device

Our apps may save small amounts of information using your device's local storage, purely so your progress and preferences aren't lost between sessions — things like:

- High scores, saved progress, or level completion
- In-app settings such as sound or vibration preferences

This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us, to the app store, or to any third party. It is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its data through your device settings.

## 4. Third-party services

Our apps do not integrate third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash-reporting tools, and make no network requests during normal use. If this ever changes for a specific app (for example, if a future release adds optional online features), we will update this policy and clearly note the change for that app.

## 5. Permissions our apps request

Any permissions a specific app requests (for example, vibration for haptic feedback) are listed on that app's individual Google Play store page. None of our apps use requested permissions to collect or transmit personal data.

## 6. Children's privacy

Our apps are suitable for a general audience and do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your region), because they do not collect information from anyone at all.

## 7. Data sharing and sale

Since no data is collected, none is shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party, advertiser, or data broker.

## 8. Your rights

Because our apps store nothing about you outside your own device, there is no external record for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. You can erase all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the relevant app or clearing its storage via your device's app settings.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future app or update introduces optional online features, analytics, or advertising — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. We recommend checking back periodically if you have concerns.
